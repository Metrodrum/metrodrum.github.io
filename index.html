Metrodrum – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

In short: Metrodrum does not collect any personal data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Everything you make in the app stays on your device, or in your own iCloud if you turn on iCloud sync.

What is saved on your device

Your settings, saved beats, drum sheets, patterns, programmes, practice log and practice results. You can delete them at any time in Settings → Your data → Delete everything and reset settings. One small note stays on the device after that: whether the free 2-minute trainer try has been used.

Files you import or share

PDF and MusicXML files you import are read on your device. Imported PDF sheets are kept in your own iCloud Drive when iCloud sync is on. When you share a sheet (PDF, ZIP or MusicXML) or a beat, the iOS share sheet sends it only where you choose (for example AirDrop, Messages or Mail); nothing is sent to us.

iCloud sync

If iCloud sync is on (Settings → iCloud sync), the app keeps a copy of the data above in your own private iCloud Drive storage so your iPhone and iPad match. This copy is held by Apple under your Apple ID and Apple's privacy policy. We cannot see it. You can turn sync off at any time; "Delete everything" also deletes it from iCloud.

Band mode

Band mode lets one phone host a "room" on your local Wi-Fi and other phones nearby join it, so every phone clicks together. No computer or account is needed. The room name you choose can be seen by other phones on the same Wi-Fi. Only clock timing and metronome settings (tempo, time signature, accents, start/stop) are exchanged between the phones, and only on that local network. No sound, recordings or personal information are sent, and nothing goes to us or to the internet. iOS asks your permission before the app uses the local network.

Reminders

The daily practice reminder is scheduled on your device. No data is sent anywhere.

Subscriptions

Pro subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Email metronomedrum@gmail.com.

Metrodrum – 私隱政策

最後更新：2026年11月22日

簡單來說：Metrodrum 不會收集任何個人資料。我們沒有伺服器、沒有帳戶、沒有分析工具，也沒有廣告。你在 App 裡建立的一切都只會留在你的裝置上；如果你開啟了 iCloud 同步，則會存放在你自己的 iCloud。

保存在你裝置上的資料

你的設定、已儲存的節奏、鼓譜、練習組合、練習計劃、練習紀錄及練習結果。你隨時可以在「設定 → 你的資料 → 刪除所有資料並重設」中刪除。刪除後裝置上只會保留一項小記錄：是否已用過 2 分鐘的免費訓練試用。

匯入或分享的檔案

你匯入的 PDF 和 MusicXML 檔案只在你的裝置上讀取。開啟 iCloud 同步時，匯入的 PDF 鼓譜會存放在你自己的 iCloud Drive。當你分享鼓譜（PDF、ZIP 或 MusicXML）或節奏時，iOS 分享選單只會把檔案傳到你選擇的地方（例如 AirDrop、訊息或郵件），不會傳送給我們。

iCloud 同步

如果開啟了 iCloud 同步（設定 → ICLOUD 同步），App 會把上述資料的副本保存在你自己的私人 iCloud Drive 空間，讓你的 iPhone 和 iPad 保持一致。這份副本由 Apple 根據你的 Apple ID 及 Apple 的私隱政策保管，我們無法看到。你隨時可以關閉同步；「刪除所有資料」也會一併刪除 iCloud 上的副本。

樂隊模式

樂隊模式讓一部手機在本地 Wi-Fi 上開設「房間」，附近其他手機加入後，每部手機會同步打拍。不需要電腦，也不需要帳戶。你所取的房間名稱，同一 Wi-Fi 上的其他手機可以看到。手機之間只交換時鐘時間及節拍器設定（速度、拍號、重音、開始／停止），而且只在該本地網絡內。不會傳送任何聲音、錄音或個人資料，也不會傳送任何資料給我們或互聯網。App 使用本地網絡前，iOS 會先徵求你的同意。

提醒

每日練習提醒在你的裝置上設定，不會傳送任何資料。

訂閱

Pro 訂閱經由 Apple 購買及管理。付款由 Apple 處理，我們不會看到你的付款資料。App 只會向 Apple 查詢你的訂閱是否有效。

兒童

本 App 不會收集任何人的資料，包括兒童。

政策更新

如本政策有更改，新版本會連同新日期在此頁公佈。
